Option A — Tab Strip
Familiar pattern — same as Spotify, YouTube, mobile browsers
One tap to switch sections — very fast navigation
Animation at top — always visible, single shared stage

Option B — Bottom Sheet
Native iOS feel — bottom nav is the dominant mobile app pattern
Maximum content area — ActivityArea fills the full screen
Animation in the sheet — each section's animation is a reveal moment
Two interactions — tap nav to reveal sheet, tap tool to launch
Best thumb ergonomics — everything reachable from bottom
